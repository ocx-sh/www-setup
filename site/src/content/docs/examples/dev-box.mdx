## See what the installer prints {#output}

On a developer machine, run the one-liner for your shell from [Install OCX](/install/#install).
This is the whole output of the POSIX installer, recorded on a fresh x86-64 Linux user.
The installer prints the first six lines and the closing message.
`ocx self setup` prints what lies between: its progress and a table of what it changed.
The version is the release that was current when the recording was made.
